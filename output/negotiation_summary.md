# Total Event Planning Negotiation Summary

## Loop 1 Negotiation Drama
The negotiation process was characterized by intense, clashing demands. Bob and Eve acted as polar opposites, with Bob demanding meat-only and proximity to David, while Eve insisted on a window seat away from all carnivores. Charlie further complicated matters by explicitly demanding to sit next to Alice, directly contradicting Alice's previous wish to avoid him.

The Host ultimately moved from a two-table to a three-table solution to maintain peace. By isolating the carnivores (Bob and David) and the vegan (Eve) into separate zones, and forcing a compromise where Alice sits with Charlie to fulfill his non-negotiable request, the host achieved a workable layout, though one that required overriding Alice’s initial preference to avoid Charlie.

## Loop 2 Negotiation Drama
The negotiation process was highly contentious due to the mutually exclusive seating demands of the guests. Alice and Charlie held irreconcilable requirements regarding their proximity to one another, while Fiona insisted on sitting exclusively next to Alice. The initial attempt to use a 'buffer' seating arrangement at a shared table failed completely, drawing sharp criticism from Alice, Fiona, and Charlie, who all felt their non-negotiable terms were violated.

To address this, the host ultimately abandoned the single-table approach and implemented a three-table configuration. While this successfully satisfied Alice's requirement to remain separated from Charlie and Bob, as well as Fiona's request for exclusive proximity to Alice, it essentially forced a compromise where Charlie's demand to be seated next to Alice could no longer be honored. The carnivore/vegan divide was resolved by segregating the guests by dietary needs across the three tables.

## Loop 3 Negotiation Drama
The negotiation process was initially mired in conflicting demands, specifically regarding the seating triangle of Alice, Fiona, and Charlie. Alice demanded to be near Fiona but away from Charlie, while Charlie insisted on being near Alice. Additionally, the clash between the strict carnivore, Bob, and the strict vegan, Eve, created a tense standoff, with Eve refusing to be in the same vicinity as a carnivore. 

After initial objections from Alice, Charlie, and Eve regarding the draft layout, the host orchestrated a comprehensive compromise by moving to a four-table configuration. By isolating parties into distinct zones, the host successfully separated Alice and Charlie, ensured Fiona was seated exclusively with Alice, kept the carnivores apart from the vegan, and satisfied Charlie's need for a dedicated, gluten-free environment. All guests are now accommodated under this multi-table arrangement.
