The negotiation process was highly contentious, primarily driven by incompatible seating mandates and conflicting dietary philosophies. Guests like Bob and Eve demanded absolute spatial separation based on dietary lifestyle, while Alice and Charlie insisted on proximity constraints that directly contradicted the Host’s initial circular table draft. The Host faced significant pushback after the first attempt, with Alice, Bob, Charlie, and Eve all filing formal objections citing feelings of neglect and safety violations.
