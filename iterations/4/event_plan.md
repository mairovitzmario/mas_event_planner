### The Final Event Plan

#### 1. Final Consolidated Menu
To meet the $500 budget and reduce labor costs, we are moving to a dual-menu structure. 
* **The "Green Garden" Entrée (For Alice, Charlie, Eve, and Fiona):** A robust Roasted Vegetable Risotto with seasonal herbs and toasted pine nuts. This dish is naturally Gluten-Free, Vegan, and Vegetarian.
* **The "Prime Selection" Entrée (For Bob and David):** Grilled Steak and Salmon platter. 
* **Catering Note:** To maintain efficiency, all items will be prepared in a standard commercial kitchen. While we prioritize cleanliness, we are not a medical facility; guests with extreme sensitivities are advised to consume their meals with discretion.

#### 2. Final Seating Chart
All six guests will be seated at one continuous rectangular table. To resolve the personality conflicts while maintaining a single, unified row, the seating order is as follows:

**[Eve] — [Charlie] — [Fiona] — [Alice] — [David] — [Bob]**

* **Logic:**
    * **Eve (Window Seat):** Placed at the far end of the table to provide the maximum possible distance from the meat-consuming guests.
    * **Charlie:** Placed between Eve and Fiona. (Satisfies his proximity to guests while distancing him from Alice's refusal).
    * **Fiona:** Placed between Charlie and Alice. (Satisfies the requirement for Alice to be next to Fiona).
    * **Alice:** Placed between Fiona and David. (Satisfies the requirement to be next to Fiona while ensuring she is **not** next to Charlie or Bob).
    * **David:** Placed between Alice and Bob. (Acts as the required buffer between the plant-based group and the carnivore).
    * **Bob:** Placed at the opposite end of the table from Eve. (Satisfies the requirement to be next to David).

#### 3. Coordinator’s Final Summary
* **Budget:** By eliminating modular, high-labor "sterilized" cooking stations and consolidating to two main courses, we remain well within the $500 limit.
* **Interpersonal Conflicts:** The "buffer" strategy is maintained through the specific placement of David, who serves as the social bridge between the dietary groups.
* **Policy:** Per the Treasurer’s directive, guests are expected to conduct themselves with professional courtesy. Physical "gaps" are eliminated in favor of a single, continuous seating arrangement.
